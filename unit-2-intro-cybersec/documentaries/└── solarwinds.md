## Findings

### 1. The incident in my own words

The SolarWinds attack was a supply chain attack. Hackers got into the SolarWinds development process and added malicious code to Orion software updates. These updates were sent to customers between March and June 2020, so the attackers could reach many organizations through software they already trusted. The malware was called SUNBURST. 

### 2. Who was affected, and how?

SolarWinds and many of its customers were affected. Around 18,000 organizations installed the compromised Orion updates, including government organizations. The attack also had wider consequences because it became a major international cybersecurity incident and the US government later attributed the attack to Russian intelligence. 

### 3. The CIA principle

I think Confidentiality was the main target. The attackers wanted to secretly collect information and access systems instead of destroying data or asking for money. Integrity was also affected because the attackers changed legitimate software before it was sent to customers.

### 4. The attack technique - what made this one different?

A supply chain attack means attacking a company through a product or service that many other organizations use. This makes the attack dangerous because one compromised supplier can give attackers access to many customers at the same time. In this case, people trusted the Orion software update, so they installed the malicious code themselves.

### 5. How was it discovered?

The attack was discovered after FireEye noticed unusual activity connected to an employee's multi-factor authentication. The employee did not recognize the login, so FireEye started investigating and found the larger attack. This shows that unusual activity and careful investigation can be very important for finding cyberattacks. 

### 6. What could have helped?

One thing that could have helped was stronger checking of the software build process. The company could use independent verification to check that the final software matches the original source code. This could have made it harder to add malicious code during the build process.

### 7. The broader lesson

The main lesson for me is that a company can have good security and still be attacked through a trusted supplier. SolarWinds showed that software updates themselves can become part of an attack. It also showed how important it is to check and monitor the software supply chain.

### 8. My personal takeaway

After watching the documentary, I will think more carefully about software updates. I usually expect an update from a trusted company to be safe, but this attack showed that this is not always true. I think it is important to update software, but also to understand that updates can create security risks.
