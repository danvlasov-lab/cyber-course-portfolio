
# Individual Reflection: Social Engineering Tabletop Exercise

The thing that surprised me most was MFA spam. I usually think that social engineering is phishing emails and bad links. I did not think about someone calling me and pretending to be IT support and sending many login requests.

After this exercise, I will be more careful with MFA notifications. If I get a login request when I am not logging in, I will not approve it. I will reject it and tell IT support if it happens again.

I also learned that I should check suspicious messages in another way. For example, if I get an email about changing bank details, I should not call the number from the email. I should find the official number and check if the request is real.

The main thing I learned is that social engineering can use pressure and trust to trick people.
