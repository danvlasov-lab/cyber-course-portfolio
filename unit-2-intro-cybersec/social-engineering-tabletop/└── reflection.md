
# Individual Reflection: Social Engineering Tabletop Exercise

I was most surprised about the fake emails with bank details. They may look identically with regular work emails. After this exercise, I will check these emails more carefully. I will not use the phone number from the email. I'll find the real number and check the information. I've learned that social engineering can be very simple. The main thing is to always check important queries before doing anything. Attackers can use trust and pressure to deceive people.
